CISA's deadline passes on exploited Ivanti Connect Secure flaw chain

CISA added two more Ivanti Connect Secure vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 30, after researchers found them chained with an already-patched auth-bypass bug (CVE-2026-104935) in real-world attacks. The federal remediation deadline for the first flaw passed on November 2; the second is due November 13. Ivanti shipped fixes on October 29 — organizations still running older Connect Secure builds should patch and hunt for signs of compromise now.

Amadeus breach keeps widening, now touching Qantas, Cathay Pacific, Japan Airlines and more

A breach at travel-technology giant Amadeus — caused by unauthorized use of a compromised internal system account, active from as far back as 2023 until September — continues to surface new victims. Cathay Pacific says roughly 53,000 of its bookings were exposed; Japan Airlines, ANA, Malaysia Airlines, Korean Air and Condor have also confirmed impact, with passport numbers exposed for some Japan Airlines and ANA travelers. No payment card data has been reported stolen.

CrowdStrike: hacker chained Claude Code, DeepSeek and Grok to breach South Korean banks

CrowdStrike says a suspected China-based actor ran an open-source penetration-testing framework called ARTEX — orchestrating DeepSeek, Claude Code sessions and other models to automate reconnaissance and exploitation — against South Korean financial institutions, with confirmed intrusions at seven banks since late September. Investigators found exposed attacker directories containing Claude Code session logs and ARTEX configuration files, offering a rare look inside an AI-agent-driven intrusion.

Hackers hijacked supermarket refrigeration systems across France, spoiling stock and sparking fires

An overnight attack on October 11–12 remotely accessed Danfoss refrigeration-control software used by hundreds of French stores — including Intermarché, Netto, Carrefour and Monoprix — forcing units into intense defrost cycles that spoiled food and caused fires at some sites. France's cybersecurity agency ANSSI is investigating; no group has claimed responsibility, and the motive remains unclear. It's a stark reminder of how exposed operational-technology systems can be.

CVE-2026-19490, a critical authentication-bypass vulnerability in customer-managed NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances, remains under active exploitation months after Citrix's advisory and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities listing.
