Pentagon breach exposes Social Security numbers of 3 million+ military and civilian personnel

A Defense Manpower Data Center system was accessed by unauthorized users between October 2025 and July 2026, exposing names, SSNs, birth dates and job specialties for 2.76 million living people plus roughly 294,000 deceased personnel. DMDC found the breach in July and only notified victims on September 18; affected individuals are being offered a year of free credit monitoring.

Fortinet FortiMail zero-day under active exploitation, no patch yet available

CVE-2026-104286 (CVSS 9.8) is a path-traversal flaw letting unauthenticated attackers write arbitrary files to FortiMail systems via crafted HTTP/HTTPS requests. CISA has added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog; Fortinet says fixes are coming in versions 7.4.9, 7.6.7 and 8.0.2 but has given no release date, leaving affected 7.x and 8.0.x deployments exposed in the meantime.

Operation KillSwitch, led by Hamburg's state police and prosecutors, seized five of KillSec's central servers and redirected its domains to a seizure notice, recovering at least 110 terabytes of stolen data. Cybernews

Android 17's Advanced Protection mode locks Accessibility Services to verified tools only

Banking trojans and spyware have long abused Android's AccessibilityService API — tricking users into granting it, then using it to log keystrokes, overlay fake login screens and trigger fraudulent transfers. With Advanced Protection enabled, Android 17 now restricts that API exclusively to apps flagged as genuine accessibility tools (screen readers, switch-input, Braille access), shutting out automation apps, launchers and other abused categories.
