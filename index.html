CISA released six ICS advisories covering flaws in HMS Networks' PCAN Windows drivers, WAGO's Linux-based OS, Moxa's AIG-302 ThingsPro Edge gateway, and Hitachi Energy's MicroSCADA X SYS600 and RTU500 products.
