CISA orders federal patching of four actively exploited flaws, deadline today

CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 18: Synology DiskStation Manager (CVE-2024-10441), BerriAI LiteLLM, Apache Axis, and Vanderbilt's REDCap. Federal agencies had until today, November 21, to patch the Synology, Axis, and REDCap flaws, with forensic triage required under CISA's binding directive in case systems are already compromised.
