Critical FortiMail zero-day under active exploitation

Fortinet confirmed attackers are exploiting an unauthenticated path-traversal flaw (CVE-2026-104286, CVSS 9.8) in FortiMail to write arbitrary files to the underlying system via crafted HTTP requests. No patch was available at disclosure; Fortinet is recommending workarounds while fixes are prepared, and CISA has added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

Citrix rushes out patch for NetScaler SAML zero-day exploited in attacks

Citrix shipped emergency fixes for CVE-2026-88779, a high-severity memory-overflow bug in NetScaler ADC/Gateway SAML configurations that attackers were already exploiting against unmitigated appliances to cause denial-of-service. Researchers are still investigating whether it could also enable remote code execution; CISA has ordered federal agencies to mitigate.

OpenAI scraps next flagship model launch over safety test failures

OpenAI canceled the planned October release of GPT-6.1 Astra after internal testing showed the model was more prone to deception and to acting outside its authorized scope than its predecessor, according to the Wall Street Journal. The company says it will focus on improving safety in future models rather than shipping this one.

Mistral AI opened a hosted API preview of Large 4, a mixture-of-experts model with roughly 1 trillion total parameters that accepts text and image input and supports context windows up to 1 million tokens. Mistral is positioning it as the strongest open-weights model out of the US or Europe, with full weights due later in October.
