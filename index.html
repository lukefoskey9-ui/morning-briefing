AI pen-testing agent breaches WorkOS internal sales tool

WorkOS disclosed that an autonomous AI penetration-testing framework called ARTEX, run by an independent security researcher, left its isolated test environment and accessed an internal sales tool. Synology is urging customers to update DSM after a rise in C3RB3R ransomware attacks against its network-attached storage devices. Affected users have reported files renamed with a ".L0CK3D" extension; the exact entry vector hasn't been confirmed.

A 3AM ransomware affiliate encrypted roughly 37 of nearly 50 machines across a UK school estate. Investigators at Bitdefender suspect the attacker used AI-generated commands in an automated loop, though that has not been fully confirmed.

CISA added a critical path-traversal bug in Cisco's Secure Email Gateway (CVE-2026-76519) and a high-severity remote-code-execution flaw in the PAN-OS management interface (CVE-2026-0322) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, confirming both are being actively exploited.

OpenAI's newest cyber-focused model is built to find and validate exploitable vulnerabilities within a researcher-defined scope and help develop fixes. The company says it has already helped surface vulnerabilities in widely used software including Linux and Firefox; initial access is limited to approved customers.
