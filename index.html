Attackers chain three Ivanti VPN zero-days for full root access

Volexity found that a threat actor tracked as UTA0576 chained three previously unknown flaws in Ivanti Connect Secure VPN appliances — including authentication-bypass CVE-2026-104935 — to run commands as root on fully patched devices, re-exploiting the appliance for every command and tampering with logs to cover its tracks. Ivanti has patched two of the three bugs; CISA has ordered federal agencies to remediate.

Cisco Secure Email Gateway zero-day exploited to execute commands as root

Cisco disclosed CVE-2026-76519, a 9.8-severity flaw in its Secure Email Gateway appliances that lets an unauthenticated attacker gain root access via a crafted email attachment. It's the second Secure Email Gateway bug exploited in the wild since mid-September; CISA has added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with no workaround available.

GitLab patches second maximum-severity, unauthenticated code-execution flaw this month

GitLab shipped fixes for CVE-2026-110264, a CVSS 10.0 flaw in the same regular-expression component as an earlier critical bug patched on October 12. Both could let an unauthenticated attacker run code on self-managed GitLab servers; admins on Community and Enterprise Edition are urged to update immediately.

Microsoft's record October Patch Tuesday includes an actively exploited zero-day

Microsoft fixed more than 1,400 CVEs this month, its largest Patch Tuesday on record, including CVE-2026-96951, a Windows Shell flaw Microsoft confirms is being exploited in the wild. CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with an October 16 federal remediation deadline.

Cyberattack knocks grocery giant Ahold Delhaize's brands offline for over a week

Ahold Delhaize confirmed "malicious actions of others" behind an October 12 cyberattack that forced it to take down websites and apps for Stop & Shop, Hannaford, and Albert Heijn across the US and Europe. US sites came back online more than a week later; the company has reported the incident to Dutch police and cybersecurity authorities.

23andMe's successor settles with California for $6 million over genetic data breach

Chrome Holding Co., the entity that emerged from 23andMe's bankruptcy, agreed to pay California $6 million to resolve state claims that it failed to protect genetic and personal data in the 2023 breach affecting roughly 6.9 million people. The deal, filed in bankruptcy court, is pending judicial approval and includes no admission of liability.
