Cisco ISE zero-day scores a perfect 10 — and it's already being exploited

A critical authentication-bypass flaw (CVE-2026-76460) in Cisco Identity Services Engine lets an unauthenticated attacker send a crafted request to reach root-level command execution. Cisco found it while resolving a customer support case, confirming at least one victim was hit before the advisory even shipped; there's no workaround, and CISA has given federal agencies just three days to patch.

The Hacker News

Gyazo breach exposes 23.6 million user records

Japan's Helpfeel disclosed unauthorized access to its Gyazo screenshot-sharing service, leaking emails, password hashes, device IDs and session IDs for roughly 23.6 million accounts, plus 490 million image-metadata entries. No payment data was taken; the company has closed the access route and is telling every user to reset passwords, including anywhere they reused them.

Hackers demand $3M from Revolut after posing as government officials

An extortion group calling itself "iamnotavillain" says it used a compromised Italian government email system to submit fraudulent law-enforcement data requests that Revolut approved, pulling records on roughly 680 customers across 30+ countries. The group is now demanding 6,000 Monero (~$3M) within 24 hours; Revolut says it hasn't paid and hasn't been contacted directly.

Mandiant reports an attacker took over an active AI coding-assistant session, stole GitHub OAuth tokens from it, and used them to push the self-propagating Shai-Hulud worm into roughly 100 repositories — a fresh reminder that agentic dev tools are becoming a supply-chain attack surface in their own right.

Researchers show a single browser extension could hijack AI assistants

A new disclosure outlines five methods a malicious browser extension could use to hijack AI assistants embedded in Chrome, Comet, Edge, Opera Neon and Claude — manipulating what the assistant sees or does without the user noticing. No CVE is listed on the US KEV catalog yet and no in-the-wild abuse has been confirmed, but it's a preview of where browser-AI attacks are heading.

OpenAI is piloting a ChatGPT ad format where clicking a sponsor's ad opens a branded AI conversation instead of an external website. Wayfair and Angi are the first advertisers live, with the agent able to answer follow-up questions before handing off to the brand's own site or service flow.

GlobeNewswire

Mistral and Mozilla team up on private AI browsing in Firefox

Mistral is now powering Firefox's "Smart Window" AI browsing assistant (beta), with a focus on privacy and multilingual support: conversations aren't stored on Mozilla's servers by default, and Mistral has committed to zero data retention. It launches first in North America and France, with the UK and Germany to follow.

Google opens the smart home to third-party AI agents

Google launched early access to a Home Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, letting outside AI agents — including Claude and ChatGPT, alongside Google's own Antigravity — control Nest and Matter devices directly. It's gated to Google Home Premium subscribers in the US for now, but marks a real crack in the walled-garden approach to smart homes.

OpenAI Foundation commits $125M+ to open scientific datasets for health AI

OpenAI's nonprofit arm launched "Public Data for Health," funding nonprofits and universities to build and preserve high-quality scientific datasets — from drug-discovery records to epidemiology and cancer-vaccine data — for researchers to use freely. It's framed as tackling AI's real bottleneck in biology: not model capability, but the scarcity of rigorous real-world data.
