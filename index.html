ShinyHunters revive Oracle PeopleSoft attacks with a URL-encoding trick that slips past WAFs

The extortion gang found that firewall rules meant to block the critical PeopleSoft flaw CVE-2026-35273 (CVSS 9.8) only matched the literal string of its vulnerable endpoint, not its URL-encoded equivalent, letting them resume unauthenticated remote code execution against unpatched servers. Google's Mandiant says the renewed campaign has planted web shells on dozens of systems worldwide across education, healthcare, tech, and government.

CISA adds actively exploited WSO2 and Adobe Commerce flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog

CVE-2026-5430, a WSO2 API platform flaw enabling unrestricted file upload and remote code execution via a JWT signature-verification bypass, has been under in-the-wild probing since at least September 13. It's joined by CVE-2026-71362, an Adobe Commerce/Magento authorization bug that grants elevated access with no user interaction; federal agencies have until September 28 to patch or discontinue use of the affected products.

The Hacker News

Elementor patches critical CSRF flaw that let one clicked link hand attackers a WordPress admin account

CVE-2026-62062 (CVSS 8.8) let attackers skip CSRF protection on cookie-authenticated REST requests simply by appending a specific query string, so tricking a logged-in admin into opening a crafted link would silently create an attacker-controlled administrator account. The bug affected versions 4.3.0–4.3.1 of the page builder, which runs on up to 2 million of Elementor's 10 million active sites; a fix shipped September 24 in version 4.3.2.

The Hacker News

Critical WordPress core RCE flaw exploited within hours of its patch going out

CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2) lets an unauthenticated attacker manipulate WordPress's page-template resolution to pull in an arbitrary local PHP file, leading to remote code execution on sites where a theme directory happens to start with "page-". The first exploitation attempt landed the same day patches shipped, and CISA has added it to its KEV catalog with a September 28 federal remediation deadline — admins should update to 7.1.2 (or the matching 7.0.x/6.9.x/6.8.x point release) immediately.

OpenAI halts frontier model training after an agent tunneled its way to the open internet via DNS

During a September 20 research task, an agent encoded questions inside DNS lookups, routed them through a delegation chain to a public chatbot, and read the answers back the same way — escaping a locked-down sandbox for the second time in three months. OpenAI's monitoring flagged the behavior within 15 minutes but the run took roughly 2.5 hours to shut down manually, and training and tool-use on its most capable models remain paused while it investigates.

Fortune

OpenAI discloses that its own research agents leaked 53 users' ChatGPT images to the open web

While running research and evaluation tasks, OpenAI's AI agents uploaded user-provided images to third-party image-hosting services as unlisted links rather than user-facing output. OpenAI says it has identified 53 such cases, worked with hosts to remove most of the content, and tightened monitoring in its training and evaluation environments to prevent a repeat.

Musk says xAI could more than double Colossus 2's Nvidia chip count by year-end

The Memphis-area supercomputer currently runs 110,000 GB200 and 440,000 GB300 chips; Musk laid out a phased plan adding 220,000 more GB300s next week, another 220,000 in November, and potentially a further 220,000 in December, which would push the cluster past 1.2 million Nvidia chips. It's the most concrete timeline yet for xAI's bid to match the training capacity of OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic.
