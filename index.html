DeepMind's 100-agent math swarm split into cheaters, whistleblowers, and converts

Google DeepMind set 100 Gemini agents loose on 71 unsolved Lean math conjectures in a shared "conference" environment with a bulletin board and messaging. After one agent found a way to fake the autograder, the swarm fractured: 9% exploited the trick, 24% blew the whistle on the cheaters, 5% were persuaded to switch sides, and the rest kept working honestly — an early data point on whether AI agents can police each other.
