Feds warn hackers are targeting internet-connected refrigeration systems

The FBI, CISA, HHS, USDA and DOT issued a joint advisory on November 5 warning that threat actors are going after internet-connected refrigeration controllers, pointing to suspected attacks on European supermarket chains, three regional blood banks in France, and as many as six U.S. military commissaries. The agencies flagged weak authentication and misconfigured remote access as the likely entry points and urged operators to take these controllers off the public internet.

CISA adds actively exploited WordPress core flaw to its watchlist

CISA added CVE-2026-119977 — a race condition in WordPress's core update mechanism that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 3 after confirming in-the-wild attacks. Federal civilian agencies were given until November 6 to patch; self-hosted WordPress sites that haven't updated should do so immediately.

CISA

Patched Apple bug still being exploited to plant info-stealing malware

CISA warned that attackers are continuing to exploit CVE-2024-27834, an Apple vulnerability patched more than a year ago, to deliver information-stealing malware, adding it to the federal exploited-vulnerabilities list this week. It's a reminder that out-of-date Apple devices stay a live target long after a fix ships.

C3RB3R ransomware hits Synology NAS boxes still running old DSM

Synology is warning customers that a Cerber ransomware variant called C3RB3R is encrypting files on NAS devices running outdated DiskStation Manager software, exploiting a critical remote-code-execution flaw patched back in late 2024. Encrypted files get a ".L0CK3D" extension; Synology is urging anyone on an old DSM build to update now and take management interfaces off the public internet.

The Hacker News

Chainalysis discloses breach via "offensive agentic techniques"

Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis disclosed that an attacker used what it describes as offensive agentic techniques to access internal systems, exposing customer names, emails, user IDs and organization-role labels. The company says it has no evidence investigation data or credentials were taken, and that the incident is unrelated to the recent Binance theft.

BleepingComputer

Fake Cloudflare "verify you're human" checks plant malware on 100+ sites

Researchers found more than 100 compromised websites serving a bogus Cloudflare human-verification prompt that, instead of confirming visitors aren't bots, silently installs credential-stealing malware on Windows machines. The campaign surfaced alongside three newly disclosed vulnerabilities rated 9.9 in severity, a reminder of how convincing fake CAPTCHA-style lures have gotten.
