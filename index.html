Dutch cyber agency warns of imminent attacks on two critical Check Point VPN flaws

The Netherlands' NCSC says exploitation of CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103 — a certificate-validation flaw and a heap overflow in Check Point's Security Gateway VPN, each rated 9.8 — is likely soon even though no public proof-of-concept exists yet. BleepingComputer

Attackers chain two JFrog Artifactory bugs to mint admin tokens and plant Rust backdoors

Between August 15 and September 8, multiple threat actors combined CVE-2026-42018 and CVE-2026-42016 to escalate an anonymous session to a full admin token — in some cases in under five minutes — then installed malicious Groovy plugins and a Rust-based backdoor for persistence. CISA has since added the flaws, along with bugs in ConnectWise ScreenConnect and MikroTik RouterOS, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

Help-desk impersonators are using fake "passkey setup" calls to raid Microsoft 365 accounts

Microsoft says actors linked to ShinyHunters and other extortion crews have spent months calling or texting employees, posing as internal IT and claiming a passkey, MFA, or SSO update is urgently needed. Victims are steered to phishing pages that steal session tokens via device-code or adversary-in-the-middle flows, after which attackers quietly register their own MFA methods and siphon data from SharePoint, OneDrive, and Exchange Online.

SecurityWeek

Google patches a Chrome V8 zero-day already being used to break out of the browser sandbox

CVE-2026-85046, a type-confusion bug in Chrome's V8 JavaScript engine, let attacker-crafted web pages corrupt memory and gain arbitrary read/write access, setting up sandbox-escape code execution. Google shipped the fix in its September 3 Stable Channel update, and CISA gave federal agencies until September 18 to patch.
