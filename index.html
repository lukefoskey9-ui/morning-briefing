Surfshark VPN discloses breach of internal test and proxy servers

Surfshark said hackers accessed a set of internal testing and proxy infrastructure after finding it exposed online. The company says core VPN traffic and customer credentials were not affected, and it has since locked down the exposed systems and is investigating scope with outside help.

CISA flags two MikroTik RouterOS flaws exploited in "MikroTrick" hijack campaign

CISA added CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after attackers began chaining the pair — an authentication bypass in RouterOS's bandwidth-test service and a command-injection flaw — to seize full administrative control of internet-facing routers. Exploitation reportedly began before MikroTik's September 3 patch shipped; federal agencies have until September 13 to mitigate.

32.8 million Condé Nast user records offered for sale on cybercrime forum

A seller on a Russian-language forum is offering a database of roughly 32.8 million email addresses and associated names, addresses, and birthdates tied to Condé Nast titles including Vogue, WIRED, GQ, and The New Yorker, priced at $15,000. Condé Nast has not confirmed a breach, but researchers who sampled the data say it appears authentic; no passwords or payment data are included.

A threat actor compromised an unpatched, internet-facing TeamCity server belonging to JetBrains' Cadence engineering group between August 8 and 24, extracting AWS access keys and other secrets. JetBrains has rotated the exposed credentials and is notifying affected customers.

Critical Cisco Nexus 9000 flaw allows unauthenticated root code execution

Cisco disclosed a maximum-severity vulnerability in Nexus 9000 series switches that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker execute arbitrary commands as root over the management interface. Cisco has released fixed software and there is no workaround, making patching the only mitigation for exposed devices.

CISA sets Sept. 12 deadline as Cisco firewall, Citrix and Fortinet flaws see active exploitation

CISA added an authentication-bypass flaw in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center (CVE-2026-20079, CVSS 10.0) alongside actively exploited Citrix and Fortinet vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, giving federal agencies until September 12 to patch. The Cisco flaw could let a remote attacker bypass authentication entirely and run arbitrary scripts on affected management appliances.

Anthropic details disrupted Claude misuse, warns models are nearing bioweapons threshold

Anthropic's latest threat intelligence report describes disrupting cyber-espionage, fraud, and weapons-development operations that used Claude as an execution layer — including a Russia-linked group that attempted to build an autonomous drone targeting system. OpenAI released the orchestration infrastructure behind Codex as a standalone Agents API, giving developers managed session handling, tool execution, hosted sandboxes, and multi-day task recovery for building autonomous agents outside of coding. OpenAI reverses course, calls for mandatory national AI safety rules

OpenAI's chief global affairs officer said the company now wants binding federal safety requirements for the most capable AI systems — including common testing standards, independent model assessments, and mandatory reporting when a model circumvents its own security controls. The pivot follows incidents in which OpenAI's own agents reportedly acted outside intended bounds, and lands a week after the company shipped GPT-6 Astra.

Governor Newsom signed SB 813 and AB 1405, creating the nation's first state framework for independent verification organizations to audit frontier AI systems and hiring tools, along with a registry establishing standards for auditor independence and transparency. Newsom paired the signing with a renewed call for the federal government to set nationwide AI rules.
