CISA confirms PHP2Shell flaws now under active attack, sets Oct. 29 deadline

CISA added CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 — two critical PHP interpreter bugs (CVSS 9.2) patched on October 21 — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after reversing an earlier "not yet observed" assessment. The pair allows unauthenticated remote code execution and is being chained into a technique researchers call PHP2Shell; federal civilian agencies have until October 29 to patch.

Microsoft's October Patch Tuesday fixes a record 1,400+ flaws, including an exploited zero-day

Microsoft's largest-ever monthly release addressed more than 1,400 vulnerabilities — around 70 rated critical and 317 enabling remote code execution — alongside three zero-days, one of which (a Windows Shell security-feature bypass) Microsoft confirms has already been exploited in the wild. Researchers note the sheer volume is straining patch-prioritization efforts industry-wide.

Dark Reading

Unpatched Tailscale SSH flaw lets a standard login escalate straight to root

Researchers disclosed an unpatched issue, dubbed "Tailworm," in which ordinary SSH port forwarding lets a non-privileged session reach Tailscale's root-level process and connect to a root-only socket — in one test, enough to read the password file. No fixed version or vendor bulletin exists yet; the only mitigation currently is disabling SSH port forwarding where possible.

French energy company ENGIE confirmed attackers stole customer data after breaching a marketing platform used by business partners, rather than ENGIE's own infrastructure. Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a natural-language-steerable world simulator

Genie 4 generates interactive, navigable 3D environments that users can reshape in real time by typing instructions — changing weather, adding objects or people, or redirecting the scene — while Gemini models handle reasoning to keep events coherent and Gemini Omni renders visuals and physics. OpenAI says it will stop training models directly on the live internet

Citing the unpredictability and risk of pulling directly from the ever-changing live web, OpenAI said future model training will instead draw on cached and simulated snapshots of internet content. The company frames the shift as a safety and reliability measure rather than a change driven by licensing or legal pressure, though it affects how fresh real-world information can be before it reaches a model.

Nvidia is investing $3 billion and AMD $1 billion into the offering; Nvidia also said the deal expands Meta's planned Vera Rubin chip deployment and deepens collaboration on Muse, Meta's AI agent platform.

Anthropic donates its Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation

Following the same path it took with the Model Context Protocol, Anthropic transferred the Agent Skills name, GitHub organization, and agentskills.io domain to the Linux Foundation-hosted Agentic AI Foundation.
