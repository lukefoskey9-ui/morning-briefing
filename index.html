Google launches prototype TPU satellites as part of its orbital AI compute bet

Google's Project Suncatcher sent four Trillium-generation TPUs into orbit on October 1 aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9, a survivability check ahead of a planned 2027 test with Planet Labs of the high-bandwidth laser inter-satellite links a real orbital compute cluster would need. Google envisions eventual ~81-satellite clusters roughly a kilometer across, betting that launch costs falling below $200/kg by the mid-2030s could make space-based compute cost-competitive with terrestrial data centers.

Data Center Dynamics

Cloudflare open-sources Clef, a "decision model" that skips text generation entirely

Clef (27B) and Clef-flash (9B), built on Qwen bases and released under Apache 2.0, run a single non-autoregressive forward pass to score typed options directly rather than generating and parsing text — cutting Clef-flash's median latency to 38.8ms versus 524.1ms for a comparable text-based model. Cloudflare is pitching the approach for cheap, low-latency routing and classification steps inside larger agentic workflows, and both models are available on Hugging Face.
