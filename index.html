Belarusian cloud provider ActiveCloud knocked offline by ransomware

ActiveCloud, a major cloud and hosting provider in Belarus, disclosed a ransomware attack on November 10-11 that encrypted parts of its infrastructure, taking down support systems, hosted sites, corporate email and customer virtual machines across Belarus and Russia. Most services were restored by November 13, and the company says it found no evidence customer data was stolen.
