Ahold Delhaize cyberattack keeps major US and European grocery chains offline

A cybersecurity incident disclosed October 12 forced Ahold Delhaize to shut down websites and online ordering for Stop & Shop, Food Lion, Hannaford and Giant in the US, plus European banners including Albert Heijn and Delhaize. In-store shopping has continued and the company says it has found no evidence customer data was stolen, but ecommerce and delivery remain disrupted more than a week later.
