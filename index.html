CISA orders rapid patching after four more flaws hit active exploitation

CISA added Synology DiskStation Manager, BerriAI LiteLLM, Apache Axis, and Vanderbilt REDCap to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 18, giving federal agencies until November 21 to remediate three of the four. Two of the bugs have already been tied to ransomware and cryptomining intrusions, and the Apache Axis 1.x flaw has no patch available since that branch is end-of-life.
