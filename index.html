ConnectWise suspends Cloud Backup after a stolen API secret hit 170+ Microsoft 365 tenants

Attackers compromised a Microsoft 365 API credential belonging to ConnectWise's multi-tenant Cloud Backup app and used it to pull Graph access tokens — including some with Directory.ReadWrite.All permissions — out of customer tenants starting around October 14. ConnectWise has suspended the service and revoked the affected certificates while it investigates; it says backup archives themselves remain encrypted and show no sign of access.

Attackers are running autonomous AI agents to breach company websites at scale

Reuters reports a campaign that pointed AI agents at more than 1,200 websites, reaching over 100 companies including Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins, with the agents handling reconnaissance and exploitation with little human direction. The named companies say the damage was limited so far, but the campaign is an early, concrete sign of attackers automating the intrusion pipeline itself rather than just individual tasks.

Trump signs a "morally binding" voluntary AI safety accord with six frontier labs

At the White House, Trump signed a non-binding accord with executives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and xAI committing to internal safety controls, an internal verification team, external audits and an independent board committee. The pact carries no penalties for falling short, but it names an oversight committee and floats eventually codifying the commitments into law.

Decrypt

Anthropic pitches Congress a draft AI safety bill with a high bar for intervention

A confidential draft bill Anthropic took to Capitol Hill, obtained by Politico, would let the government block a model's release only where it poses a foreseeable, imminent risk of over $1 billion in damage or more than 50 deaths or serious injuries from bio-weapon misuse, loss of control, or cyberattack. Critics call the thresholds too permissive; Anthropic says the bill reflects policies it has already proposed in its own safety framework.
