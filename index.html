PHP ships emergency patches for critical "PHP2Shell" RCE flaws

Two unauthenticated remote-code-execution bugs (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both rated 9.2 on CVSS) affect nearly every PHP deployment that serves web requests, including PHP-FPM, mod_php, php-cgi and FrankenPHP. Disclosure details leaked early, forcing the PHP team to rush out fixes on October 21; scanners have since turned up more than 22 million potentially exposed instances. Patch to 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35 now.

Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a proactive AI agent
