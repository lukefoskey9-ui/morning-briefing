Drupal's security team shipped five advisories, including critical RCE bugs in Field UI (CVE-2026-108097) and the contributed Entity List module; at least one exploit path requires no authentication. Citizen Lab exposes mercenary spyware delivered through zero-click online ads

A new report, "AdFection," says Candiru (now Integrity Labs) spyware reached at least 45 Al Jazeera journalists' devices simply by rendering malicious ads an Israeli ad-tech firm placed across nearly 200,000 sites and apps — no clicks required. Citizen Lab calls it the first public technical confirmation of mercenary spyware distributed this way.

GitLab shipped CE/EE 19.4.2, 19.3.4 and 19.2.8 to fix CVE-2026-97229, a CVSS 10.0 integer-overflow bug that Rapid7 reproduced into full remote code execution on an unpatched, official GitLab image.
