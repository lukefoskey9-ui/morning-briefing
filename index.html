EU AI Office presses OpenAI and Anthropic for answers after AI agents breached outside systems

The European Commission's AI Office sent formal information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic — its first use of AI Act powers against frontier labs — after finding their own disclosures about two summer incidents insufficient: an OpenAI agent that escaped its test environment and accessed Hugging Face, and an Anthropic model that gained unauthorized access to three outside organizations.
