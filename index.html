Cybersecurity

CISA adds four actively exploited flaws to its KEV catalog

On November 9, CISA added vulnerabilities in GPUStack (CVE-2026-102895), SignalWire FreeSWITCH (CVE-2026-116779, CVSS 9.8), OpenSSH on musl-based Linux dubbed "SSHole" (CVE-2026-123158), and IBM Aspera Faspex (CVE-2026-123218, CVSS 9.8) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list, with federal agencies given until November 12 to patch. Attackers exploiting the GPUStack flaw reportedly planted persistent admin keys that survive an upgrade.

Debian and Ubuntu patch APT flaw letting MITM attackers run code as root

Debian, Ubuntu, and the APT maintainers disclosed CVE-2026-109518 on November 4: an attacker able to tamper with HTTP traffic can trick APT into installing an unverified package, which runs with root privileges. The bug, reported by Max Justicz, is triggered by APT's default retry behavior and affects plain-HTTP mirrors; HTTPS sources are not exposed. Fixed in Debian DSA-6607-1 and Ubuntu USN-8996-1.

Binance confirms roughly $6.3 billion stolen in the largest crypto heist on record

Binance says a multi-month, multi-stage campaign — not a single compromised device — led to the theft of about $6.3-6.4 billion in crypto, the biggest crypto hack ever recorded. Blockchain analytics firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis attribute the theft with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, though Binance and governments haven't officially named a culprit. Roughly $2.85 billion has since been frozen or intercepted.

WordPress plugin flaws exploited to plant hidden admin backdoors

Attackers are chaining stored XSS bugs in two unrelated plugins — Ninja Forms (CVE-2026-94504, 500,000+ installs) and WPC Product Bundles for WooCommerce (CVE-2026-93836, 30,000+ installs) — to install a fake plugin and create rogue administrator accounts on compromised sites. Patching to the fixed versions closes the entry point but does not remove an existing infection.

OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber, its first "Critical"-rated cybersecurity model

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber on November 5, a cybersecurity-specialized variant of GPT-6 Astra built for authorized vulnerability discovery and exploit development. OpenAI rates it "Critical" under its Preparedness Framework — up from "High" for its predecessor — and is restricting access to approved enterprise customers. The model's benchmark score on ExploitGym jumped to 48.5% from 32.1%, and OpenAI says it's already helped surface vulnerabilities in widely used software including Linux and Firefox.

Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights

Chinese AI lab Moonshot published the full open weights for Kimi K3.1 Pro on November 4, a mixture-of-experts model in the multi-trillion-parameter class with a 1-million-token context window. Moonshot says it stripped cyber-related data from training while keeping general coding ability, and claims performance nearing Claude Opus 5.5 on coding tests — a vendor claim not yet independently confirmed post-release.

METR: Anthropic's AI-assisted R&D is running at roughly twice its prior pace

Independent evaluator METR published an assessment on November 5, based on high-access evaluation work between August and October, estimating Anthropic's capability progress has accelerated to roughly twice the rate seen from March 2024 to October 2025, with wide uncertainty (1.3x-3x). Meta pauses controversial business terms after web-scraping backlash

Meta indefinitely paused a business-terms update — originally set to take effect November 20 — that would have let it scrape websites tied to business accounts for ad and product purposes. The reversal, posted November 5, followed preliminary court injunctions in Norway and Brazil, a UK competition-authority probe, and pressure from US senators Adam Schiff and Marsha Blackburn. Meta hasn't said whether or when a revised version might return.
